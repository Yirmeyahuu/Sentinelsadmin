About the System

This platform is the SENTINELS Admin and Superadmin's panel — the control center for managing all users and monitoring activity in the SENTINELS mobile game.

Superadmin

Managed by the Information Technology Support Service (ITSS)

  • Full access to manage all Faculty and Student accounts
  • Oversees the entire system's user management and settings

Admin (Faculty)

Authorized Faculty members who teach Cybersecurity Fundamentals

  • Manage Student accounts, activities, and progress
  • Responsible for classroom oversight and guidance

Core Features

User Management

  • • Create, Read, Update, Archive (CRUD) Student accounts
  • • Create and manage Faculty accounts (Superadmin only)
  • • Secure login for both Admin and Superadmin roles
  • • Role-based access control for proper permissions

Game & Activity Management

  • • Create and customize cybersecurity activities
  • • Assign activities to specific classes or groups
  • • Set deadlines for activities and tasks
  • • Upload instructions, media, and reference materials

Monitoring & Analytics

  • • Real-time progress monitoring from Firebase data
  • • Interactive charts and analytical insights
  • • View leaderboard standings and performance
  • • Export progress reports to PDF or Excel

Classroom Management

  • • Organize students into sections or groups
  • • Assign group-based challenges
  • • Track participation rates and completion times
  • • Monitor overall classroom performance
