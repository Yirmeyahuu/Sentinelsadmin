SENTINELS. A 2D role-playing game designed for students at STI West Negros University - College of Information and Communications Technology. This educational game helps learners understand cybersecurity fundamentals by immersing them in real-world scenarios based on course handouts. Players will explore topics such as cybersecurity roles and responsibilities, threats and vulnerabilities, and types of hackers while engaging in interactive challenges. The game offers three difficulty levels—Novice, Junior, and Senior—each featuring three tasks/activities and an intense boss battle to test their knowledge. Fun, engaging, and educational, this game makes learning cybersecurity an exciting adventure—play now and become a cybersecurity expert!
